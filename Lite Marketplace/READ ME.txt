LITE MARKETPLACE (made in Plain)
================================
Create your online store in minutes and sell on WhatsApp.
First made by Wilson Makafui as a website (HTML + Firebase), now a Plain app.


WHAT'S IN THIS FOLDER
---------------------
Lite Marketplace.ui   The app for sellers. Open it in Plain and press F5.
Shop.ui               The shop customers see. Publishing turns it into a
                      web page (you don't need to open it yourself).

Lite Marketplace makes these the first time you use it:
marketplace.json      sellers, products, orders and payments
settings.json         the Flutterwave secret key. Keep this file private.
photos\               product photos
shop.json             the shop that was published last
web shops\            every seller's shop as a web page (index.html)


HOW IT WORKS
------------
1. Sign up: email, password, WhatsApp number and a store name.
   The first account is the admin (it gets the Admin tab).
   Passwords are never saved: only a hash of them (hash of salt + password).
2. My products: name, price, category, description, "In stock", and a
   photo ("Choose photo"). Click a product in the list to change or delete it.
   The Free plan has room for 5 products. A paid plan: as many as you like.
3. Orders: write down the orders you get on WhatsApp, and mark them Paid,
   Delivered or Cancelled. The Dashboard shows your sales and a chart of
   the last 7 days.
4. Publish: makes your shop into one web page (web shops\<your shop>\index.html).
   It works on any phone: customers search, fill a cart, type their name and
   where to deliver, and press "Order on WhatsApp". The order arrives on your
   WhatsApp as a message.
   To share it, upload index.html to a free host:
     - Netlify Drop: go to app.netlify.com/drop and drag the folder there
     - or GitHub Pages
   Then paste the address in the Publish tab and press "Share on WhatsApp".
   Publish again whenever you change your products, and upload the new page.
5. Plans: Weekly GHS 30, Monthly GHS 100, 3 Months GHS 230, 5 Months GHS 400.


PAYMENTS WITH FLUTTERWAVE
-------------------------
1. Log in to your Flutterwave dashboard > Settings > API keys.
2. Copy the SECRET key. Start with the TEST secret key (FLWSECK_TEST-...),
   so no real money moves while you try it.
3. In Lite Marketplace, log in with the admin account, open Admin > Settings,
   paste the key and press "Save settings".
4. A seller presses Subscribe: Flutterwave's payment page opens in the
   browser (Mobile Money or card). After paying, the seller presses
   "I have paid: check my payment". Lite Marketplace asks Flutterwave whether
   the payment went through (and that it is the right amount, in GHS), then
   switches the plan on until its end date. A plan that runs out goes back to
   Free when the seller logs in.
5. When everything works with the TEST key, put in your LIVE secret key.

Keep the secret key private: it is saved in settings.json on this computer
only. Don't share that file or put it on the web. (Your published shop page
never contains it, or anyone's password or email.)

Admins can also give a seller a plan by hand (for cash or Mobile Money paid
directly): Admin > click the seller > pick the plan > "Give this plan".
